Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 2026

Data we collect

Your dietary preference selections are stored locally on your device only. We do not store or share your personal data on any external server.

Camera

Camera access is used only to scan menu images. Photos are sent to our AI analysis service for processing and are not stored.

Advertising

Free tier users see ads served by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect anonymised data for ad personalisation in accordance with Google's privacy policy.
